# Master Services Agreement

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between Vertex Analytics S.r.l., an Italian limited liability company ("Provider"), and the customer identified in the applicable Service Order ("Customer"). Provider and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". This Agreement governs every Service Order executed by the Parties during its term, and each Service Order incorporates this Agreement by reference whether or not it says so expressly.

## Article 1 — Definitions

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms have the meanings set out below, and cognate expressions are to be construed accordingly. "Affiliate" means, with respect to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with that Party, where control means ownership of more than fifty percent (50%) of the voting securities. "Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a public holiday in Milan, Italy, and any period expressed in days that does not specify Business Days is computed in calendar days.

"Confidential Information" has the meaning given to it in Article 11, and the label attached to a document is not decisive for its classification. "Deliverables" means the reports, configurations, models, and documentation identified as deliverables in a Service Order, excluding in every case the Provider Tools. "Documentation" means the user documentation for the Services that Provider makes generally available, as updated from time to time. "Fees" means the amounts payable by Customer under a Service Order, determined in accordance with Article 5.

"Provider Tools" means the software, libraries, scripts, know-how, and methodologies owned by Provider or its licensors before the Effective Date or developed outside the scope of the Services, together with every improvement to them, and the term extends to any derivative created during the performance of the Services to the extent it embodies those tools. "Service Levels" means the availability and response commitments described in Article 7. "Services" means the data engineering, analytics enablement, and managed platform services described in a Service Order.

In this Agreement, a reference to a statute includes that statute as amended; the words "including" and "in particular" do not limit the generality of what precedes them; headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; and words in the singular include the plural and vice versa. Where a period is expressed to run from a notice, it runs from the day after the day of receipt, and where it ends on a day that is not a Business Day, it is extended to the next Business Day.

## Article 2 — Scope of Services and Service Orders

Provider shall perform the Services described in each executed Service Order with reasonable skill and care, in accordance with the practices of a competent professional services firm operating in the same field, and in material conformity with the Documentation. Each Service Order must identify the Services, the Deliverables, the Fees and the invoicing model, the term of the engagement, and the names of each Party's engagement managers; a Service Order that omits one of those elements is nonetheless valid if the omission can be cured by reference to this Agreement.

No Service Order binds Provider until it has been signed by an authorized signatory of Provider. Purchase orders, vendor onboarding forms, and similar Customer documents are for administrative convenience only, and any terms printed on them are expressly rejected even if Provider acknowledges or processes the document. Changes to an executed Service Order require a written change order signed by both Parties describing the modified scope and any adjustment to the Fees or the timeline, provided that Provider may make immaterial adjustments to the manner of performance without a change order when they do not reduce the substance of the Services.

Customer shall provide, at its own cost, the cooperation reasonably necessary for the performance described above, including timely access to systems, data, environments, and knowledgeable personnel. Provider is not responsible for a delay or a failure to the extent caused by Customer's failure to cooperate, by the inaccuracy of materials supplied by Customer, or by a third party outside Provider's control engaged by Customer; in each such case the affected milestones are adjusted by the time reasonably attributable to the impediment, and any additional effort demonstrably incurred is chargeable at the rates of the applicable Service Order.

Where the performance requires the processing of personal data on Customer's behalf, the data processing addendum executed by the Parties applies and prevails over this Agreement for that processing. Each Party shall appoint and keep appointed one engagement manager with authority to take the day-to-day decisions required by this Article, and shall replace that person with a substitute of comparable seniority upon reasonable notice to the other Party.

## Article 3 — Term of the Agreement

This Agreement enters into force on the Effective Date stated in the first Service Order executed by the Parties and continues for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months. At the end of the initial term it renews automatically for successive renewal periods of twelve (12) months each.

Renewal does not occur if either Party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current period. Notice of non-renewal is subject to the form requirements of Article 15 and does not, by itself, affect any Service Order then in force.

The expiry or termination of this framework does not by itself terminate any Service Order then in force: each Service Order continues until its own expiry or termination in accordance with its terms, and the provisions of this Agreement survive for as long as necessary to govern it. Provisions which by their nature are intended to survive, including Articles 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, and 13, survive the expiry or termination of this Agreement for any reason.

## Article 4 — Termination

Each Party may bring this Agreement, or any individual Service Order, to an end without cause. The right of the preceding sentence is exercised by written notice given at least forty-five (45) days before the intended effective date, and, where a Service Order with a committed term is concerned, its exercise does not relieve Customer of the obligation to pay the unpaid portion of the committed Fees, which becomes due in full on the effective date stated in the notice.

A Party may also end this Agreement, or the affected Service Order, with immediate effect by written notice where the other Party commits a material breach and, the breach being capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within thirty (30) days of receiving a written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail and stating the intention to terminate. A failure to pay under Article 5 is deemed material for these purposes when the unpaid amount exceeds one monthly invoice and remains unpaid fifteen (15) days after a written reminder. The same immediate right arises, to the extent permitted by applicable law, where the other Party becomes insolvent, is subject to bankruptcy or composition proceedings, enters liquidation, or ceases to carry on business.

On the effective date of any ending under this Article, Customer shall pay all Fees accrued and unpaid up to that date, Provider shall deliver the Deliverables completed and paid for, and each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information in accordance with Article 11. Amounts already invoiced remain due according to their original terms, and any credit accrued under Article 7 that has not been applied to an invoice lapses without compensation.

Where the ended Service Order concerns the managed platform, Provider shall in addition, at Customer's written request made within the following thirty (30) days and at the rates then in force, provide reasonable assistance for an orderly handover, including the export of Customer Data in a commonly used machine-readable format and up to two knowledge-transfer sessions with the incoming operator. The assistance of this paragraph is not available while undisputed amounts remain unpaid.

## Article 5 — Fees, Invoicing, and Payment

The Fees for each Service Order are stated in that Service Order and, unless it provides otherwise, are invoiced monthly in arrears for consumption-based components and monthly in advance for committed subscription components. All amounts are stated net of value added tax and of any other applicable indirect tax, levy, or withholding, all of which are borne by Customer where legally chargeable, except taxes on Provider's income. Travel and subsistence expenses are chargeable only when the Service Order provides for them and are invoiced at cost against receipts.

Each invoice is payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice, without set-off, deduction, or counterclaim, except for amounts disputed in good faith under the procedure of this Article. Amounts not paid when due and not so disputed bear late-payment interest at the rate provided by Italian Legislative Decree 231/2002, and Provider may suspend the Services affected by the non-payment upon ten (10) Business Days' prior written warning, without prejudice to Article 4.

A Customer that considers an invoice incorrect, in whole or in part, must notify Provider in writing within fifteen (15) days of the invoice date, describing the objection and paying the undisputed portion when due. The Parties shall then cooperate to resolve the matter within thirty (30) days, failing which either of them may refer it to the escalation procedure of Article 14; no suspension right may be exercised over the portion genuinely in dispute while the procedure is pending.

Unit rates may be revised once per contract year with effect from the renewal of the Service Order concerned, upon at least ninety (90) days' prior written notice. The revision may not exceed the greater of five percent (5%) and the percentage change of the Italian FOI consumer price index over the preceding twelve months, and a revision notice that arrives late takes effect only at the following renewal. No revision applies to a committed component during its committed term.

## Article 6 — Customer Data and Security

As between the Parties, Customer retains all rights in the data, content, and materials that Customer or its users submit to the Services or make available to Provider ("Customer Data"), and nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of Customer Data to Provider. Customer grants Provider a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to host, copy, transmit, process, and display Customer Data solely to the extent necessary to provide the Services, to comply with law, and to exercise the rights expressly granted in this Agreement. Customer is responsible for the lawfulness, accuracy, and quality of Customer Data and warrants that it holds the rights and consents necessary for the processing contemplated by the Services.

Provider shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in the security annex to the applicable Service Order, which must in any event include encryption of Customer Data in transit and at rest, role-based access control under the principle of least privilege, logging of administrative access, and a tested incident response process. Provider personnel access production environments only through individually attributed accounts, and shared credentials are prohibited.

If Provider becomes aware of a confirmed compromise of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or unauthorized disclosure of Customer Data, it shall inform Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware. The communication shall describe the nature of the incident, the categories and approximate volume of data affected, the measures taken or planned, and a contact point, and shall be supplemented as the investigation progresses.

Provider may produce and use aggregated and anonymized statistics derived from the operation of the Services, provided that they identify neither Customer, nor any natural person, nor any Customer Confidential Information; such statistics are Provider Tools. Upon Customer's written request, no more than once per contract year, Provider shall make available a summary of its most recent third-party security audit, subject to the confidentiality obligations of Article 11.

## Article 7 — Service Levels and Service Credits

For managed platform Services, Provider commits to a monthly availability of ninety-nine point five percent (99.5%), measured per calendar month at the boundary of Provider's infrastructure. Excluded from the computation are: scheduled maintenance announced at least five (5) Business Days in advance and performed within the agreed windows; emergency maintenance necessary to address an imminent security risk; unavailability caused by Customer's systems, configurations, or third-party services outside Provider's control; and events under Article 13.

Where the measured value in a calendar month falls below the commitment of the first paragraph, Customer is entitled, as its sole and exclusive remedy for the shortfall, to a credit equal to five percent (5%) of the monthly fee of the affected Service for each full percentage point of shortfall. The credits of this Article are capped, in each calendar month, at thirty percent (30%) of the monthly fee concerned; they are applied to the next invoice, have no cash value, and lapse with the termination of the Service Order.

A request is required for any credit: it must be made in writing within thirty (30) days of the end of the month concerned, and it is otherwise forfeited. Provider's availability measurements prevail unless Customer demonstrates, by records of at least equivalent reliability, a materially different value for the same period.

Support tickets are classified at opening. A report of a production outage without workaround is acknowledged within one (1) hour on Business Days between 08:00 and 20:00 Central European Time, with progress updates at intervals not exceeding four (4) hours until a workaround or resolution is provided. Reports of severity high, normal, and low are acknowledged within four (4) Business Hours, one (1) Business Day, and two (2) Business Days respectively, with the target resolution effort described in the Documentation. A failure to meet the acknowledgement time of the first sentence in three (3) consecutive months entitles Customer to end the affected Service Order under Article 4 as if it were an unremedied material breach.

## Article 9 — Indemnification

Provider shall defend Customer against any third-party claim alleging that the Services or the Deliverables, as delivered by Provider and used in accordance with this Agreement, infringe a patent, copyright, or trademark enforceable in the European Union, or misappropriate a trade secret, and shall indemnify Customer against the damages, costs, and reasonable attorneys' fees finally awarded by a court of competent jurisdiction or agreed in a settlement approved by Provider in writing.

If such a claim is made or is, in Provider's reasonable opinion, likely to be made, Provider may at its own expense procure for Customer the right to continue using the affected item, or replace or modify it so that it becomes non-infringing without materially reducing its functionality. If neither option is reasonably available, Provider may end the affected Service Order and refund the Fees paid for the affected item in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

The obligations of this Article do not arise for a claim caused by a combination of the Services or Deliverables with items not supplied by Provider where the claim would not have arisen without the combination, by a modification not made by Provider, by Customer Data, or by use in breach of this Agreement or the Documentation. Customer shall defend and indemnify Provider, under the same procedural conditions, against third-party claims arising from Customer Data or from Customer's use of the Services in breach of law or of this Agreement.

The Party seeking defence must notify the other Party promptly in writing of the claim, grant it sole control of the defence and settlement, and provide reasonable cooperation at the defending Party's expense; a delay in notification relieves the defending Party only to the extent it is prejudiced by it. This Article states the entire liability of Provider, and the exclusive remedy of Customer, for the infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.

## Article 10 — Limitation of Liability

Nothing in this Agreement excludes or limits a Party's liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence, for death or personal injury caused by its negligence, for fraud, or for any other liability that cannot be excluded or limited under applicable law. Nothing in this Article limits Customer's obligation to pay the Fees due, and the caps below do not apply to the confidentiality obligations of Article 11 to the extent the breach consists of an intentional disclosure.

Subject to the first paragraph, neither Party is liable to the other for loss of profits, loss of revenue, loss of anticipated savings, loss of business opportunity, or for any indirect or consequential loss, even if advised of the possibility of it. The loss or corruption of data is compensated only up to the cost of restoring the data from Customer's most recent backup, it being understood that Customer maintains backups with a frequency appropriate to the criticality of the data.

Subject to the two preceding paragraphs, the aggregate liability of each Party arising out of or in connection with this Agreement and every Service Order, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, is limited, for all events occurring in any period of twelve (12) consecutive months, to one hundred percent (100%) of the Fees paid or payable by Customer for the Services concerned in that period. For the obligations of Article 9 only, the preceding cap is raised to two hundred percent (200%) of that amount. The Parties acknowledge that the Fees reflect this allocation of risk and that Provider would not contract on these commercial terms without it.

Any action arising out of this Agreement must be commenced within twenty-four (24) months of the date on which the Party bringing it became aware, or ought reasonably to have become aware, of the facts giving rise to it; the limitation of this paragraph does not apply where a longer period is mandatory under applicable law.

## Article 11 — Confidentiality

"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement that is marked confidential, that is identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances, including the terms of this Agreement, the Provider Tools, security information, product roadmaps, and business and financial information. It does not include information that is or becomes publicly available without breach of this Agreement, that the receiving Party already lawfully possessed without a duty of confidence, that it receives from a third party without breach of a duty of confidence, or that it develops independently without use of the disclosing Party's information.

The receiving Party shall use the disclosing Party's Confidential Information only for the purposes of this Agreement, shall protect it with at least the degree of care it uses for its own information of a similar nature and never less than reasonable care, and shall limit disclosure to its personnel, Affiliates, and professional advisers who need it for those purposes and are bound by obligations no less protective. The receiving Party remains responsible for every person to whom it discloses under this paragraph.

Disclosure required by law, by a court, or by a competent authority is permitted to the extent required, provided that, where legally permitted, the receiving Party gives the disclosing Party prompt notice and reasonable assistance to seek protective measures, and discloses only what is required. Publicity, press releases, and the use of the other Party's name or logo require prior written consent, except that Provider may identify Customer as a customer, without describing the engagement, once the first Service Order has been executed.

Upon written request following the end of this Agreement, each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, except for copies retained in routine backups or where retention is required by law, which remain subject to this Article for as long as they are retained. The obligations of this Article survive for five (5) years after the end of this Agreement and, for trade secrets, for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

## Article 12 — Intellectual Property and Licences

Provider retains all right, title, and interest in the Provider Tools, and no provision of this Agreement transfers them to Customer. Customer grants Provider, for the term of the applicable Service Order, the licences to Customer's systems and materials strictly necessary for the performance of the Services. Feedback, suggestions, and ideas provided by Customer about the Services may be used by Provider without restriction or obligation, and they are not Customer Confidential Information.

The rights in each Deliverable pass as follows: upon full payment of the Fees due for it, Provider assigns to Customer the intellectual property rights in that Deliverable, to the extent they are capable of assignment and always excluding the Provider Tools embedded in it; moral rights, where inalienable, are unaffected. Until that payment, Customer holds a revocable licence to use the Deliverable internally for evaluation only.

For Provider Tools embedded in a Deliverable, Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use them as part of that Deliverable, for Customer's internal business purposes, without the right to extract them, commercialize them separately, or make them available to third parties other than service providers acting on Customer's behalf under confidentiality obligations consistent with Article 11.

Software identified in a Service Order as open source is provided under its own licence terms, which prevail over this Agreement for that software. Provider shall identify in the Documentation the open-source components whose licences require the distribution of source code, and shall not embed in a Deliverable a component whose licence would oblige Customer to disclose Customer proprietary code, unless the Service Order authorizes it expressly.

## Article 13 — Force Majeure

Neither Party is liable for a failure or delay in the performance of its obligations, other than payment obligations already accrued, to the extent caused by an event beyond its reasonable control, including natural disaster, epidemic, war, terrorism, civil unrest, embargo, act of a public authority, widespread failure of telecommunications or power infrastructure, or a general strike. A shortage of personnel internal to a Party, a price increase of a subcontractor, or a market condition making performance more onerous but not impossible does not qualify.

The affected Party shall notify the other without undue delay, describing the event and its expected duration, shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate the effects, and shall resume performance as soon as reasonably possible. The time for performance is extended by a period equal to the duration of the event and its direct consequences, and the other Party's corresponding obligations, other than accrued payment obligations, are suspended for the same period.

Where an event of this Article prevents the performance of a material part of a Service Order for a continuous period of more than sixty (60) days, either Party may end the affected Service Order by written notice with effect from its receipt. In that case Customer pays the Fees accrued up to the date on which performance was suspended, and Provider refunds any Fees prepaid for Services not performed.

This Agreement and any non-contractual obligation arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of Italy, excluding its conflict-of-law rules and excluding the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. The courts of Milan, Italy have exclusive jurisdiction, without prejudice to either Party's right to seek urgent interim or protective measures before any competent court.

Proceedings on the merits may not be commenced before the internal path of this paragraph has been followed: within ten (10) Business Days of a written escalation notice, the engagement managers shall discuss the dispute; failing resolution, the matter is referred to a director of each Party, who shall meet within a further fifteen (15) Business Days; failing resolution within thirty (30) days of the escalation notice, either Party is free to act. The path of this paragraph does not apply to the collection of undisputed amounts, to urgent interim relief, or to the protection of intellectual property rights or Confidential Information.

If a provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, it is reformed to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable while preserving the Parties' intent, and the remaining provisions continue in full force. The English language version of this Agreement prevails over any translation prepared for convenience.

Formal notices under this Agreement, including notices of breach, of ending under Article 4, and of non-renewal, must be in writing and delivered by hand, by registered mail with return receipt, or by certified electronic mail (PEC) to the addresses stated in the Service Order, and are effective upon receipt. Operational communications may be exchanged by ordinary email between the engagement managers, but they never constitute formal notice.

Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either Party may assign it to an Affiliate or to the acquirer of all or substantially all of its business, upon written notice, provided the assignee assumes all obligations and, for an assignment by Customer, is not a direct competitor of Provider. Provider may subcontract parts of the Services to subcontractors bound by obligations consistent with this Agreement, remaining responsible for their performance as for its own; the subcontractors authorized to process personal data are listed in the data processing addendum.

This Agreement, together with its annexes and the executed Service Orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes every prior agreement, representation, and understanding, written or oral. A waiver is effective only if in writing and only for the instance for which it is given, and the failure to exercise a right is not a waiver of it. Amendments require a written instrument signed by both Parties, and the same applies to the amendment of this sentence.

In the event of conflict, the order of precedence is: the executed Service Order for its specific commercial terms; the data processing addendum for the processing of personal data; this Agreement for everything else; and the Documentation for technical descriptions. The provisions of Articles 1, 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 14, and this Article survive the end of this Agreement to the extent necessary to give them effect.
