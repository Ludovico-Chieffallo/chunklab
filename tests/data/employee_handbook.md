# Acme Corporation Employee Handbook

This handbook describes the policies and procedures that apply to all employees of Acme Corporation. It is provided for evaluation purposes and is a fictional document released into the public domain. The handbook is reviewed annually by the People Operations team, and the most recent revision supersedes all previous versions. Employees are expected to read the handbook in full during their first week of employment and to direct any questions to their manager or to People Operations.

## 1. Employment Terms

### 1.1 Employment Classification

Acme Corporation classifies employees as full-time, part-time, or temporary. Full-time employees work at least thirty-five hours per week and are eligible for the complete benefits package. Part-time employees work fewer than thirty-five hours per week and receive prorated benefits. Temporary employees are hired for a fixed term of no more than twelve months and are not eligible for benefits except where required by law.

### 1.2 Probationary Period

All new employees serve a probationary period of ninety days from their start date. During the probationary period, either the employee or the company may end the employment relationship with seven days' written notice. Successful completion of the probationary period is confirmed in writing by the employee's manager.

### 1.3 Termination and Notice

After the probationary period, either party may terminate the employment relationship by providing written notice at least 30 days prior to termination. The company may waive the notice period and provide payment in lieu of notice at its sole discretion. Termination for gross misconduct may be immediate and without notice. Upon termination, employees must return all company property, including laptops, badges, and confidential materials, no later than their final working day.

## 2. Compensation and Hours

### 2.1 Working Hours

Standard working hours are nine to five, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Employees may agree flexible arrangements with their manager, provided that core hours of ten to four are covered and the total weekly hours are maintained.

### 2.2 Overtime

Non-exempt employees who work more than forty hours in a single week are entitled to overtime compensation. Overtime is paid at 1.5x the regular hourly rate for all hours worked beyond forty in a week. Overtime must be approved in advance by the employee's manager. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay but may receive time off in lieu at the company's discretion.

### 2.3 Payroll

Salaries are paid monthly on the last business day of the month by direct deposit. Payslips are available through the employee self-service portal. Any payroll discrepancies must be reported to People Operations within thirty days of the payment date.

## 3. Leave Policies

### 3.1 Annual Leave

Full-time employees accrue twenty-five days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to five unused days may be carried over into the first quarter of the following year. Leave requests must be submitted through the portal at least two weeks in advance for absences of three days or more.

### 3.2 Sick Leave

Employees receive ten days of paid sick leave per year. Absences of more than three consecutive days require a medical certificate. Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on termination.

### 3.3 Parental Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to sixteen weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken in up to two separate blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must notify People Operations at least eight weeks before the intended start of leave.

## 4. Data and Security

### 4.1 Data Retention

Customer records are retained for seven years after the end of the customer relationship, after which they are securely deleted. Employee records are retained for six years after the end of employment. Backup archives are encrypted and rotated on a ninety-day cycle.

### 4.2 Acceptable Use

Company equipment and networks are provided for business purposes. Reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties or violate any policy. Employees must not install unlicensed software, share credentials, or connect unauthorized devices to the corporate network. All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled.

### 4.3 Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents, including phishing attempts and lost devices, must be reported to the Security team within twenty-four hours of discovery. The Security team operates an on-call rotation and can be reached through the internal hotline at any time.

## 6. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to act with integrity, treat colleagues with respect, and avoid conflicts of interest. Gifts from vendors exceeding a value of one hundred dollars must be declared to the employee's manager. Violations of the code of conduct are investigated by People Operations and may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Conflicts of interest arise when an employee's personal interests could improperly influence, or appear to influence, business decisions. Common examples include holding a significant financial interest in a supplier or competitor, hiring or supervising a close relative, and accepting paid work from a company partner. Employees must disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest to their manager in writing as soon as they become aware of it. Disclosed conflicts are reviewed by People Operations, which may put safeguards in place such as reassigning approval authority or recusing the employee from a specific decision.

Outside employment is permitted provided it does not interfere with the employee's duties, involve a competitor of the company, or make use of company equipment, confidential information, or working hours. Employees planning to take a second job or a substantial advisory role must notify their manager in advance.

## 7. Remote and Hybrid Work

Acme Corporation operates a hybrid working model. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, with office attendance expected on team anchor days agreed within each department. Fully remote arrangements are available only for roles designated as remote-eligible in the job catalogue and require written approval from both the department head and People Operations.

Remote employees are expected to maintain a safe and ergonomic workspace, a reliable internet connection, and availability during core hours of ten to four in their team's primary time zone. The company provides a one-time home office stipend of five hundred dollars for eligible employees, which may be spent on a desk, chair, monitor, or peripheral equipment. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within sixty days of purchase.

Employees working remotely from another country, even temporarily, must obtain prior approval from People Operations because of tax and employment law implications. Working abroad without approval for more than ten business days in a calendar year is treated as a policy violation.

## 8. Travel and Expenses

Business travel must be booked through the corporate travel portal and approved in advance by the employee's manager. Economy class applies to all flights under six hours; premium economy may be booked for longer flights. Hotel bookings should not exceed the nightly cap for the destination city published in the travel portal.

Reimbursable expenses include transportation, lodging, meals during travel, conference fees, and client entertainment approved in advance. Expense reports must be submitted within thirty days of the expense being incurred, with itemized receipts attached for any expense over twenty-five dollars. Per diem meal allowances are seventy-five dollars per day for domestic travel and one hundred dollars per day for international travel. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment and never for employee-only meals.

Personal credit card points earned on business travel may be retained by the employee. Mileage for use of a personal vehicle on company business is reimbursed at the prevailing national rate published each January.

## 9. Performance and Development

Performance reviews are held twice per year, in June and December. Each review covers goal attainment, demonstration of company values, and a development plan for the following period. Ratings are calibrated across departments to ensure consistency, and the outcome is shared with the employee in a one-to-one meeting with their manager.

Promotions are considered during the December review cycle. A promotion case requires a written nomination from the employee's manager, evidence of sustained performance at the next level for at least two quarters, and approval by the department head. Compensation adjustments linked to promotion take effect on the first of January following the review.

Every employee has access to an annual learning budget as set out in the benefits table. The budget may be spent on courses, books, conferences, and professional certifications relevant to the employee's role. Requests above the individual budget require department head approval. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the following year.

Underperformance is addressed first through informal coaching. Where concerns persist, a formal performance improvement plan of at least eight weeks is put in place, with written objectives and fortnightly check-ins. Failure to meet the objectives of a performance improvement plan may result in reassignment or termination of employment.

## 10. Equipment and IT Provisioning

New employees receive a company laptop, and where the role requires it, an external monitor and headset, on or before their first day. Laptops are refreshed on a three-year cycle. Requests for non-standard equipment are made through the IT service desk and require manager approval for items above two hundred dollars.

All company devices are enrolled in mobile device management. Employees must install security updates within seven days of release, must not disable endpoint protection, and must report lost or stolen devices to the Security team within twenty-four hours. Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mail application with a device passcode enabled.

Software is licensed centrally by the IT department. Employees must not purchase software licences on expense accounts without IT approval, and must not use personal cloud storage accounts for company data. Approved collaboration and storage tools are listed in the IT knowledge base.

## 11. Health, Safety and Wellbeing

The company is committed to providing a safe working environment. Workstation assessments are offered to all employees on request and are mandatory for employees reporting discomfort. First aid kits are located on every floor beside the kitchen areas, and trained first aiders are listed on the intranet safety page.

Employees who feel unwell during the working day should inform their manager and go home; such absences of less than one day are not counted against sick leave. Work-related injuries, however minor, must be recorded in the incident register within forty-eight hours.

The employee assistance programme provides free, confidential counselling on personal and work-related matters, available around the clock by phone and online chat. The company also reimburses up to thirty dollars per month of gym or fitness subscription costs for employees enrolled in the wellbeing programme.

## 12. Grievances and Disciplinary Procedure

Employees who believe they have been treated unfairly should first raise the matter with their manager, or with People Operations where the concern involves the manager. Formal grievances must be submitted in writing and are acknowledged within three business days. An impartial investigator is appointed, and the outcome is communicated in writing within twenty business days of the grievance being lodged, unless an extension is agreed.

The disciplinary procedure applies to conduct and performance matters not resolved informally. Depending on severity, outcomes range from a verbal warning, to a first written warning, to a final written warning, to dismissal. Employees are entitled to be accompanied by a colleague at any formal disciplinary meeting and may appeal a disciplinary outcome in writing within five business days of receiving the decision. Appeals are heard by a manager not previously involved in the case.

Examples of gross misconduct include theft, fraud, violence or threats of violence, serious breaches of confidentiality, deliberate damage to company property, and working while impaired by alcohol or drugs. Gross misconduct may result in immediate dismissal without notice following an investigation.

## 13. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Employees have access to confidential information including customer lists, pricing, product roadmaps, and personal data of colleagues and customers. Confidential information must not be disclosed to anyone outside the company, or to colleagues without a business need to know, during or after employment. Confidential documents must be stored in approved systems and must not be forwarded to personal email accounts.

All intellectual property created by employees in the course of their employment, including inventions, designs, code, and written materials, belongs to Acme Corporation. Employees must promptly disclose such creations and provide reasonable assistance in securing the company's rights, including signing assignment documents where required.

Media enquiries must be referred to the Communications team without comment. Employees must not speak to journalists, analysts, or investors on behalf of the company unless expressly authorized. Personal social media use must make clear that views expressed are the employee's own, and must never disclose confidential information.
